RULE 01 • SIMPLE Present Perfect Simple

Formation: Active & Passive

التكوين في المبني للمعلوم والمجهول للمضارع التام البسيط

Active (المبني للمعلوم)
I / You / We / They + have ('ve) + P.P.
He / She / It + has ('s) + P.P.
Passive (المبني للمجهول)
Object + have / has + been + P.P.
Active: Radwa has washed the dishes.
Passive: The dishes have been washed by Radwa.
رضوى غسلت الأطباق | تم غسل الأطباق بواسطة رضوى.
RULE 02 • CONTINUOUS Present Perfect Continuous

Formation & Ongoing Aspect

تكوين المضارع التام المستمر والتركيز على استمرار النشاط

Form (التكوين)
Subject + have / has + been + (verb + ing)
Passive Voice (المجهول - يُفضل البسيط دائماً)
Object + have / has + been + P.P.
Omar has been working on the report for three hours.
عمر مستمر في العمل على التقرير منذ 3 ساعات وحتى الآن.

Keywords, Positions & Examination Formulas

Keyword Grammatical Placement & Conditions Exemplary Construction Arabic Rationale (الشرح والقاعدة)
yet Placed at the end of negative statements and interrogative questions. "Have you arrived home yet?" للتعبير عن حدث متوقع حدوثه حتى الآن في نهاية النفي والسؤال.
just Between the auxiliary (have/has) and past participle (P.P.). "I have just returned home." للتعبير عن أحداث تمت منذ فترة وجيزة جداً (منذ لحظات).
already In affirmative sentences or in questions expressing surprise at early completion. "Has he finished the exam already? Very clever!" للتأكيد على إنجاز الفعل بالفعل، أو التعجب من سرعة الإنجاز في السؤال.
ever / never Superlative equivalence formula: the most ... ever = never ... such a "This is the best film I have ever seen." تأتي ever مع التفضيل والأسئلة، وتأتي never لنفي التجربة الحياتية العامة.
so far Affirmative sentences quantifying results or actions completed up to now. "Ayman has drunk five coffees so far." بمعنى (حتى اللحظة الحالية) وتأتي مع تراكم الكميات والأعداد المنجزة.
since Punctual start point, life event noun, or since + past simple clause. "Omar has worked here since he graduated." تحدد نقطة انطلاق الحدث، أو يتبعها جملة في الماضي البسيط.
for Duration of the entire completed time block (e.g. for 17 years, for ages). "I have played football for as long as I can remember." تحدد إجمالي المدة الزمنية المستغرقة كاملة.

Subordinating Conjunctions of Cause: because / as / since

The tense of the cause clause depends strictly on whether the result is situated in the present or the past:

Present / Future Result
"He is sad because his father has been shouting at him."
النتيجة في الحاضر (is) ← السبب مضارع تام مستمر (has been shouting).
Past Result
"He was sad because his father had been shouting at him."
النتيجة في الماضي (was) ← السبب ماضي تام مستمر (had been shouting).

Grammatical Feature Present Perfect Simple (البسيط) Present Perfect Continuous (المستمر)
Primary Semantic Focus Result & Completion:
Emphasizes the finished product, knowledge acquired, or resulting state.
"Omar has read many books on farming. He is a real expert." 		Activity & Effort:
Emphasizes the ongoing nature of the process or persistent activity over time.
"Sarah has been taking Arabic lessons since she arrived."
Quantified Items & Counts Mandatory:
Required whenever counting objects, letters, cups, or occurrences.
"I have written three reports." 		Strictly Forbidden:
Cannot take counts of completed objects.
(Never: have been writing three reports)
Momentary Verbs Valid:
Punctual, split-second actions.
"Omar has broken his leg." 		Forbidden:
Actions without duration cannot be prolonged.
(Never: has been breaking his leg)
Prolonged Activities (live, work) Verbs of long-term activity (work, live, study, teach) can be used in either the present perfect simple or continuous with "since" to convey essentially the same meaning:
"I have worked with this team since 2014." = "I have been working with this team since 2014."
